Privacy Policy – Manhwa Recaps Uploader

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Operated by the owner of the YouTube channels “Manhwa Fans” (English, youtube.com/@ManhwaRecaps6637), “Mahnwa Recaps Hindi” (Hindi, youtube.com/@MahnwaRecapHindi) and “Manhwa Recap Spanish” (Spanish, youtube.com/channel/UCpuST9oYOYRTRQG-xasQBTw) · Contact: inspiredbybuddha1@gmail.com

1. What this application is

“Manhwa Recaps Uploader” (Google Cloud project manhwa-recaps-uploader-514316) is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels “Manhwa Fans” (English, youtube.com/@ManhwaRecaps6637), “Mahnwa Recaps Hindi” (Hindi, youtube.com/@MahnwaRecapHindi) and “Manhwa Recap Spanish” (Spanish, youtube.com/channel/UCpuST9oYOYRTRQG-xasQBTw) to upload and schedule those channels’ own videos. It is not offered, sold or made available to any other person or organisation. Its only users are the three Google accounts that own those channels, all controlled by the same owner.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

This application uses the YouTube API Services. By using it, the user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy, also http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

3. What information we access

  • YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload): used only to upload our own channels’ videos (videos.insert) with a title, description, tags and a scheduled publish time, and to set their custom thumbnails (thumbnails.set).
  • Read-only channel information (youtube.readonly): used only to read our own channels’ names and IDs (channels.list, mine=true) to confirm that each upload goes to the correct channel.

We do not access, collect or process any data about other YouTube users, viewers, subscribers, comments, analytics or any other channel.

4. What we store and where

  • The OAuth access/refresh token of each of our own three channels, stored only on the channel owner’s computer in files readable only by that user. They are never shared or transmitted anywhere except to Google’s servers to make the API calls above.
  • A local log of our own uploads (video file name, the YouTube video ID returned by the API and the scheduled publish time) so the tool does not upload the same video twice.

No personal data of any other person is collected or stored. We do not use cookies, tracking, advertising identifiers or analytics in this application.

5. How we use and share information

The information above is used only to upload and schedule our own videos. It is not sold, rented, shared with or disclosed to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

6. Data retention and deletion

The upload log is kept only as long as needed to avoid duplicate uploads; log entries are deleted after 30 days. Stored YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Access can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (or https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). When access is revoked or the tool is no longer used, the stored tokens and the upload log are deleted from the computer within 7 days.

To request deletion or ask any question about this policy, email inspiredbybuddha1@gmail.com; we respond within 7 days.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new “Last updated” date.
